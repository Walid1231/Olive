## Olive 1.5.18

- Downloaded songs and audiobooks now show their real names instead of "Olive"
- Audiobook progress is exact: it shows how far you are in each chapter and how much time is left
- Continue listening shows Now playing / Paused, and tapping it plays or pauses
- New sort menu with clear icons for songs and audiobooks
- Swipe the mini player left or right to skip songs
- Now Playing takes its colours from the cover, and you can swipe the cover to skip
- The "All chapters" list is folded away so the audiobook page is tidier
- Loading screens show placeholders instead of a spinner
- Nothing is hidden behind the mini player any more
