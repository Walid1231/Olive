## Olive 1.3.1 · Polish ✨

### Feels better
- Every button now has the navigation dock's touch: a springy press and a light vibration.
- The Songs | Audiobooks, Inbox | Friends | Requests switches use the green gradient pill, like the dock.
- Glass cards have a softer, lit edge. Photo cards cast a gentle shadow.

### Friends
- The red unread badge on the Friends tab is readable again: a white ring, and no longer overlapping the label.
- New **Listen together** and **Group playlists** tiles.
- **Listening now** cards are cover photos with your friend's picture and a moving **LIVE** equalizer.

### Widget
- An **Add** button on Home (and in Library → ⚙ → **Add widget**) puts the Olive widget on your home screen in one tap.

### Updates inside the app
- Olive checks for new versions when you open it, then downloads and installs them itself. No more reinstalling from the browser.
- Library → ⚙ → **Check for updates**, any time.
- The first update asks once for "Install unknown apps". Olive takes you to the switch and continues automatically.
- Your songs, playlists and sign-in stay as they are.
