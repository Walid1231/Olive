## Olive 1.5.0 · Discover

### Search now opens with music, like Echo / YouTube Music
- Before you type, **Search** shows shelves from YouTube Music: Quick picks, new releases, Bollywood, Ghazal & Sufi, charts, artists, albums and playlists.
- **Mood chips** at the top (Relax, Energize, Romance, Party, Focus…) switch the shelves.
- Tap a song to play it (the rest of the shelf queues up after it). Tap a playlist, album or artist to open it, then play, shuffle or download any song.
- The shelves are **saved on your phone**, so they appear instantly. They're refreshed in the background.

### Faster search
- Results come from YouTube Music directly, usually in under a second (the old engine is still the backup).
- **Suggestions while you type**. Tap ↖ to fill in a suggestion and keep typing.
- The keyboard's search key searches right away.
